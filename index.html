The idea

Three layers

  1. Feel itYou don’t need a theory. Walk the three tables and notice that the same mask feels different.
  2. Who decides what it’s worth?Sacred, specimen, souvenir. None of these values came with the mask. They were arranged around it.
  3. You were framed tooYou walked into a class exhibition. We, the curators, framed you the way the tables framed the mask.
